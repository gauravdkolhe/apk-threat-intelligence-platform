# Project Abstract

The rapid growth of Android applications has significantly increased the risk of mobile malware, spyware, banking trojans, and fraudulent applications targeting individuals, organizations, and digital financial systems. Cybercriminals increasingly distribute malicious APKs disguised as legitimate applications to steal sensitive information, intercept OTPs, gain unauthorized access to devices, and establish remote communication channels through Command and Control (C2) infrastructure.

Traditional antivirus solutions primarily rely on signature-based detection methods and often fail to identify newly emerging, obfuscated, or behaviorally similar threats. Additionally, existing systems generally lack collaborative threat intelligence, behavioral correlation, and investigation-oriented analytics required by cybersecurity investigators and law enforcement agencies.

To address these challenges, the proposed project introduces an AI-Powered Collaborative APK Threat Intelligence and C2 Detection Platform that performs automated static and controlled behavioral analysis of Android applications while integrating community-driven threat intelligence and investigation support capabilities.

The platform enables users and investigators to upload APK files for analysis, where the system extracts permissions, APIs, suspicious strings, URLs, IP addresses, certificate signatures, and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) associated with malicious behavior.

The proposed solution applies AI-assisted threat scoring and behavioral analysis techniques to identify suspicious activities such as OTP interception, overlay attacks, unauthorized background communication, excessive permission abuse, credential theft attempts, and hidden network connections.

A key innovation of the system is its collaborative threat intelligence and similarity correlation mechanism. When suspicious applications are detected, their behavioral fingerprints, threat indicators, and metadata are securely stored within a centralized intelligence repository.

The system additionally provides an authority-focused investigation dashboard designed for cybersecurity analysts and law enforcement agencies. The dashboard enables authorities to monitor suspicious APK trends, increasing malware cases, linked malware clusters, regional threat patterns, recurring C2 infrastructure, and emerging attack campaigns through interactive visualization and AI-generated forensic insights.

By transforming isolated APK scans into a collaborative and intelligent cyber threat monitoring ecosystem, the proposed platform aims to improve Android malware detection, accelerate cybercrime investigations, and support proactive mobile threat intelligence and digital forensic operations.
